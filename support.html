Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a barcode?

Simply open the app and point your camera at any barcode or QR code. Scanly will automatically detect and process the code in real-time.

Where is my scan history stored?

Your history is stored locally on your device for maximum privacy. We do not sync your scans to any external servers unless you explicitly share them.

Can I create my own QR codes?

Yes! Navigate to the "Generate" tab where you can choose from various templates or create custom codes from scratch.

What is Batch Mode?

Batch Mode allows you to scan multiple codes in quick succession without stopping to view each result immediately. It's perfect for inventory or organizing.
