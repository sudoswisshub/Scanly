Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Scanly. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application.

Data We Collect

Scanly is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to our servers. All processing happens locally on your device.

  • Camera Access: Camera access is used solely to scan barcodes and QR codes. Images are processed in real-time and are never uploaded or stored by us.
  • Local Storage: Your scan history and saved codes are stored strictly on your device. We have no access to this information.
  • AI & Food Services: Any external lookups (like food products) are performed without sending any personal identifiers.

How We Use Your Data

Because we collect nothing, your data never leaves your device. Any information generated during your use of the app is used exclusively to:

  • Display results and history locally on your screen.
  • Generate barcodes and QR codes within the app environment.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to provide specific features, such as food product databases or AI analysis. These services are selected for their commitment to security and privacy.
